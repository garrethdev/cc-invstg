# Confessions: The Secret Past, Present, and Future of Money

Project brief for any Claude session in this folder. Written Oct 2026 from the working session. Update as decisions change.

## Concept
- Short-form series, formerly "Crypto Confessions", now **Confessions**. One season, eight episodes. First episode was filmed years ago; a sizzle exists (Downloads/crypto_confessions_sizzle (1080p).mp4, still old branding).
- **Real stories** from insiders (finance, financial-crime investigation), told in silhouette. Interviews are recreated with an actor where needed. The **host framing is fiction**: a masked V-for-Vendetta-style character with a meta story arc. Blend of fiction and nonfiction, Nathan Fielder / Borat in spirit.
- Goals: viewership and subscribers, product sales through the host's in-character pitches, a media moment, and a launchpad for the owner's film career (owns film/TV rights; book rights go to the journalist hire with a percentage to the owner, details still to be drafted by a lawyer).
- **Top priority (Oct 2026): the rollout is the meta movie.** The series rollout, the whistleblower hunt and real-world reactions are filmed and become the feature. See `project-map.md` section 4.
- Distribution plan: clipper program and a growth agency (Media Scaling, Logan Forsyth; guarantees 5M-500M views/year via clippers). Owner expects far more than that. Planned pilot first.

## Episode format (the setup before each interview)
1. Cold open: masked host alone in a lived-in room with theme props (oversized book, bonsai and tweezers). Talks to camera ("dear viewer").
2. Stakes line, then a jump into history (archival footage, animation).
3. Bridge to the present, then **cut to the real whistleblower interview**.
4. Closing line and on-screen text. Visual arc: the host becomes more serf-like over the short.
- Products appear **only in host segments**, never in the interview segments.
- Existing scripts: `CM_Tower_of_Basel_Project_Agora_Editing_.pdf` and `CM_Technofuedalism_.pdf` (in Downloads). Recommended pilot: technofeudalism cut to ~75s.

## Look (physical shoot; do NOT use AI image generation for the look)
Shared rules across references: one hard source, near-total black, texture in the light or air, partial reveal of the person, lots of empty frame.
- Caustic hanging glass fixture, raking rippled light, cool blue (reel reference).
- Rim-light portrait (light directly behind, face in shadow; remove fill for anonymity).
- Droplet/particle macro ("Sinner as I Am"), black void, one prismatic glint.
- Blue banded-light face, shot on Sony FX3 with a handheld FX filter in camera (reel by rogulskiproject); plain white studio, lighting plan sent to gaffer.
- Golden-hour boat interview, serif headline on top, yellow all-caps captions (reel by brittleonie): short-form template.
- Red illustrated silhouette scene (forest, beam through haze): layered depth; possible animated reenactment style.
- First take (pink cyc wall, visible studio gear) is what to move away from. Planned fix: textured/uneven colored wall, hazer, hard diagonal beam, black foreground layers, remove all visible gear, sharp silhouette edge.
- Open decision: palette. Leaning blue for interviews and magenta for the host.
