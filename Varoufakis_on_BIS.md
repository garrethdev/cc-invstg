# Yanis Varoufakis on the Bank for International Settlements (BIS)
Compiled 26 Sep 2026.

## Verified statements by Varoufakis that name the BIS
1. Project Syndicate, 28 Jul 2021, "A Central Bank Cryptocurrency to Democratize Money" (mirrored on yanisvaroufakis.eu). He notes the idea of central bank digital currencies "is gaining support from the financial establishment such as the Bank for International Settlements, known as the central bank of central banks." He argues these "financial gatekeepers" back CBDCs only to pre-empt the People's Bank of China and Big Tech, and to "usher in digital currencies that preserve the current oligarchy's monopoly over money."
2. Efimerida ton Syntakton, 10 Jun 2017 (Greek), "Lagarde and Schauble lent to Greece to save their banks". He cites BIS data as showing three French banks were so exposed to the eurozone periphery that a 3 percent loss on their exposures would have required a French state bailout: EUR 627bn to Italy, Spain and Portugal, EUR 102bn to the Greek state, EUR 562bn needed overnight. Same argument as in his 2016 book And the Weak Suffer What They Must? and Adults in the Room.
3. UnHerd, 19 Jun 2025, "The stablecoin time bomb hidden in Trump's GENIUS Act". He cites BIS Working Paper 1270 (Ahmed and Aldasoro, May 2025) for the estimate that stablecoin issuers bought about USD 40bn of T-bills in 2024, more than any foreign buyer.

## Related pieces where the BIS is the backdrop but not named
- Project Syndicate, Jun 2025, "Trump Wants Big Tech to Own the Dollar": central bankers left the IMF-World Bank spring meetings in "dread" over the GENIUS Act; the ECB is fast-tracking a wholesale CBDC.
- Project Syndicate, 12 Jul 2025, "A Trust Fund for Everyone to Defuse the Stablecoin Time Bomb": Fed digital wallets as "the mother of all stablecoins".
- The Econoclasts (UnHerd) episode 1, 29 Oct 2025, with Wolfgang Munchau: 23:15-47:17 on the crypto/stablecoin crisis. Transcript checked; the BIS is not named.

## Fake: the "emergency meeting at the BIS in Basel" video
A video circulating since late Dec 2025 has "Varoufakis" saying he was invited to an "informal economic consultation" in Basel that turned out to be an emergency meeting of about 30 central bank officials at the BIS, with Treasury auctions failing, hidden dollar dumping, capital control plans, and USD 3-5 trillion pension losses. It was amplified by e.g. Marius Ghilezan's Romanian podcast (YouTube pComjOHAtOg, 1 Jan 2026). It is AI-generated. Varoufakis wrote in The Guardian on 5 Jan 2026 ("I'm watching myself on YouTube saying things I would never say") that hundreds of deepfake videos using his face and voice have proliferated, and even a repost site (henrymakow.com, 1 Jan 2026) flagged this specific clip as AI. Nothing on his site, X account or Project Syndicate corroborates any BIS visit.

## What he has not said
No record found of Varoufakis commenting on the BIS founders, its 1930 origins, its wartime record, or its gold. His BIS references are instrumental: as a data source on bank exposures, and as the establishment body now endorsing CBDCs.
