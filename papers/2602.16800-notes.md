# Notes: "Large-scale online deanonymization with LLMs" (arXiv 2602.16800)

Lermen, Paleka, Swanson, Aerni (MATS / ETH Zurich), Carlini (Anthropic), Tramèr (ETH Zurich). PDF: `arxiv-2602.16800.pdf` in this folder. Saved 2026-10-08.

## What it shows
- An LLM agent with web search can re-identify pseudonymous users from their posts alone. Hacker News users: **67% identified at 90% precision** (226 of 338). Cost **$1–4 per person**.
- Four-step pipeline ("ESRC"):
  1. **Extract:** an LLM reads someone's posts and writes a profile: job, employer, location, age, interests, life details, both stated and inferred.
  2. **Search:** convert profiles to embeddings and find the closest candidates (e.g., LinkedIn profiles).
  3. **Reason:** a stronger LLM compares the shortlist and picks the match, or abstains.
  4. **Calibrate:** a confidence score sets how sure it must be before it names anyone.
- It works across platforms (HN ↔ LinkedIn), across communities (Reddit), and across time. Classical methods got ~0%.
- The authors deliberately did **not** publish the agent, prompts, or tool setup, to limit misuse. They list surveillance of journalists, activists and dissidents as a main risk.

## Why it matters for Confessions
- **For the Source Locator:** it confirms the approach (post history → profile → candidate match → human check). This is the forum → LinkedIn link we want.
- **Even at 90% precision, 1 in 10 matches is wrong.** For us, a wrong match means contacting the wrong person or telling someone their colleague talked. Every match needs a human check before outreach.
- **It cuts both ways.** Banks, agencies and anyone else can run the same thing on *our* sources' forum posts. Source protection has to assume that pseudonymous accounts no longer protect anyone. Coach sources not to post about the story anywhere.
- **Possible episode/meta-movie beat:** "Your anonymity is already gone." The show that protects whistleblowers in silhouette, in a world where AI can unmask anyone for $4.

## How we'd build it (decided scope)
- **Targeted, not bulk.** One flagged poster at a time (one the scanner or a tip surfaced), not "match every forum user against LinkedIn".
- Output goes to the reporter only, with confidence + evidence, then human verification, then outreach through the poster's own channel. Guardrails in `../tools-scratchpad.md` apply.
- Not built: the database-vs-database version that unmasks whole communities at once. It sweeps up thousands of people who have nothing to do with the story, creates privacy-law exposure (GDPR, LinkedIn terms), and runs against what the show stands for.
